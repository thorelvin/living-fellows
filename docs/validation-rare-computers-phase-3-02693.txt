Implemented
- Three small companion reaction pools, triggered after a persona reply has
  finished. They use the existing recruited-speaker availability and shared
  Banter/Dialogue budget. Sleeping/busy/unavailable companions are not forced
  to speak; nobody is moved, assigned a job or interrupted. The chance is 35%
  for each machine. Chance failures and delivered remarks are retained through
  normalization. Busy/unavailable speakers leave an eligible remark pending
  for a later completed reply. The CRT also shows the actual accepted speech
  because the full-screen monitor covers overhead bubbles.
- A later camp EMMA visit can acknowledge an encountered personality, one at
  a time and once per world. Blank first-open screens do not qualify. The
  supplied outcome lines wait for actual therapist relief / officer handover.
  Outside-camp, locked and unknown-camp contexts cannot consume a follow-up.
- Queueing is not delivery: the receipt commits only after the complete line
  has typed or the player explicitly skips it. Closing during boot/typing
  leaves it pending. Optional receipts are bounded to the three known IDs,
  survive saves and later first-boot Director decisions, and normalize safely
  from old saves. No engine or dialogue-data files were edited.
- Recovery Log gains a quiet hint after conversation, with no added lock kind,
  discovery checklist, secret names or change to the completion/license target.
- A read-only mounted-battery query supplies actual charge to the world
  provider and EMMA's power text. Off/depleted/broken backups do not advertise
  power. Existing grid/substation/generator behavior remains; the substation
  label retains its original priority over the square it powers.
- Persona dialogue stays as logical lines until measured display wrapping.
  Ordinary EMMA queues now also use measured glyph widths. This avoids double
  wrapping a last word onto its own line and keeps text inside the menu gutter.
- Changelog and computer docs use the handoff's spoiler-light hint. The Field
  Guide draft has the same one-line hint and its front release note is updated
  for the shipped .93 companion fixes. No rare-computer identities or location
  details are added to player-facing documentation.
