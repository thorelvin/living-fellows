[h1]0.27.5 - Getting to know your survivors[/h1]

[b]Updated for Project Zomboid 42.22.2. Single-player.[/b]

[h2]A more caring EMMA[/h2]
[list]
[*]EMMA recognizes more expressions of distress, grief and self-doubt, and responds gently. When she is unsure, she asks instead of agreeing with a harsh statement or turning it into a joke.
[*]She keeps track of the care conversation and understands short follow-up answers. Her usual jokes and lore progression pause while that context is active.
[*]Everything stays offline. Care conversations are excluded from the optional misunderstood-message log.
[/list]

[h2]Getting to know people[/h2]
[list]
[*]Talk now shows named relationship stages, progress toward the next stage and recent reasons for changes. The former Ally stage is now called Familiar.
[*]Offer food, drinking water or medical help without opening barter. Choose the exact supply to give; water clearly hands over the whole container. Helping a real need builds trust, with diminishing returns for repeated gifts.
[*]Ask household members about joining before they are ready. Personal trust, household standing, contracts, trial periods and family obligations now have separate explanations. Recruitment thresholds are unchanged.
[*]Household previews no longer change recruitment state. Hostile households stay out of the nearby Talk list, and expired gift panels close safely.
[/list]

[h2]More to talk about with EMMA[/h2]
[list]
[*]Broader conversation, better typo and slang handling, two-question answers and 26 pages of survival references.
[*]Follow up on her replies naturally. Ask about a generator, then its fuel, then where to find it. Follow-ups also cover people, relationships, equipment, jobs, expeditions, radio and building controls.
[*]Ordinary computers share operator recognition and a notebook. Recover computers for fragments and memory tiers; moving or resetting one never counts it twice. Factory reset affects that computer while preserving shared memories.
[*]Jump-start an unpowered computer with Electrical 2, a screwdriver and 5% of a car battery. The session lasts up to ten real minutes, without powering building or radio services.
[*]Improved live health, infection, calendar and weather readings. Forecasts now use the correct day-offset API instead of sometimes describing old weather as tomorrow's forecast.
[*]Survival-game questions and slang keep their ordinary meaning, without being mistaken for personal disclosures.
[*]Less repeated Knox lore, clearer answers about infection, and more ways to ask about EMMA's previous operator or her own words.
[/list]

[h2]Keeping camp work moving[/h2]
[list]
[*]Sorting keeps its place when shelves change and checks later shelves before returning to large ones. Full storage and interrupted searches have clearer pause and retry behavior.
[*]Floor pickups retain ownership of their animation until completion or a verified interruption.
[*]Hungry residents can request a supply run after an empty search. Hunger and thirst can interrupt passive mental episodes; recovery deadlines survive the interruption.
[*]Giving supplies no longer prevents later shared meals from building trust. Critical-health diagnostics now record needs, illness, fire and recent damage reasons.
[/list]

[h2]42.22.2 compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Rebuilt the native bridge for the updated game, including world-object lists, combat hit lists and the new rope-climbing entry points.
[*]Fixed an area-selection script request occurring too early during menu startup, and an adapter error when returning to the main menu.
[*]Existing LF save schemas are unchanged. Public debug spawning, movement recording and experimental actors remain disabled.
[/list]

[b]Updating:[/b] Restart the game fully after updating. Workshop requires ZombieBuddy 2.3.3 or newer with its one-time loader setup. Standalone users should run the updated Install.bat. Use one edition at a time.
