Living Fellows 0.27.5 - relationship clarity and direct help
Date: 2026-11-08
Branch: living_fellows/0.27.5

Implemented
- Talk shows Cautious, Familiar, Trusted, Close and Family, with progress to
  the next existing threshold and up to three recent relationship reasons.
- Relationship conversation is available before recruitment. Ordinary household
  residents can be focused for Talk without adding their entire household to
  the companion roster. Existing Strange Folk context conversations stay authored.
- Offer food and drinking water without requiring the recipient to have trade
  stock. The item picker checks at most 512 items across the player's inventory
  and nested bags. Incomplete searches have a different message from no supplies.
- Confirm one exact item. Water labels disclose its measured litres and that the
  whole container is being given. Medical help opens the existing native care UI.
- Retain native food safety, fresh/normal/emergency priorities, favourites,
  protected possessions and action reservations. Confirmation rechecks the
  player, recipient, need, ownership, access, safety and carrying capacity.
- Reuse the trade ownership journal, rollback and recovery for physical gifts.
  There are no scripted hunger/thirst reductions, replacement items or new scans
  while the terminal or Talk screen is idle.
- Food and water each have a rolling 24-game-hour help window: first gift full
  benefit, second half, later gifts supplies only. Portion size scales benefit.
  Care is credited at handover; later portions/drinks do not grant it again in
  that window. Optional care fields persist through the existing save schema.
- Household joining explains personal relationship versus household standing,
  contracts, cooldowns, trial status, resident obligations and authored gates.
  Existing eligibility, scores, thresholds and trial decisions are retained.
- Stage changes have a nonmodal relationship note; confirmed gifts report their
  result. Camp setup says Establish camp here, and barter confirmation says Trade.
- Release metadata and rebuilt bridge are 0.27.5. Native protocol remains
  42.21-isocompanion-30; world/inventory save schemas are unchanged.
