Scope
  1. Two-wide staircases: early climber warning on every parallel column.
  2. Doorway posts ("Guard here" on a door): the front guard stays in line with
     the opening; zombies queued behind the one in the opening are not counted
     as contacts, for every seated guard; a zombie staggered just through the
     opening is still the head of the queue, not a breach.
  3. Squad pull-out: one fit, armed rear guard (nearest the zombies; the follow
     formation's rear guard wins a near tie) covers for up to 3 s while safe,
     then follows. Unarmed or hurt members never cover.
  4. Automatic safe ground: a doorway or stair top within 4 m of the player on
     the player's floor, with every nearby zombie coming through it, taken by
     followers (nearest at the front) when the group gives ground or is
     outnumbered or even. Kept while the player stays within 5 m and no zombie
     is more than 2 m inside; dropped on pull-out. Never searched off the
     player's floor. Bounded: at most 81 squares, once a second.

Decision refined during the work (flag for the user)
  The plan said safe ground triggers "when the group must give ground". Live,
  the zombies queued outside a door do not count as arrivals until they are
  through it, so "give ground" came only after the door was lost. The trigger
  now also fires while engaging when there are at least as many zombies as
  fit fighters, all coming through the choke.
