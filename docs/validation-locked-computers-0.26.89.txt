Implemented behaviour
---------------------
- Optional schema-one lock state, bounded world ledger, first-open settling,
  legacy word-puzzle retention and conservative malformed-state fallback.
- Word/Remembers Director weights 40/60; first Remembers, then word. Owners
  do not repeat until their available category is exhausted. Twenty supplied
  owners were added: 162 profiles and 648 entries in total.
- Remembers uses six-letter words and real sentences from the owner's files.
- Open-window movement lamp, adjacent-floor attenuation, closing detection,
  3 Hz ceiling, Flash/Steady/Off setting, and health/contact exit checks.
- Confirmed alternatives, persistent CRACK.EXE progress and physical CMOS
  recovery, including battery delay, fixed seeded risk, minor burn and wipe.
- One-time XP and access stamp, owner welcome, verified physical rewards,
  desk interlock, manual drawer release, native crowbar recovery, failed-login
  history, recovery progress and nearby companion speech/hints.
- Original phosphor-tinted art and synthesized cues. Art sources, prompts,
  hashes and lossless texture partitions are recorded in terminal-lock-art.json.

Issues found and corrected during acceptance
-------------------------------------------
- Typed drawer release originally reached the generic building-unlock parser.
- Clearing an inventory callback exposed an inherited native drop handler.
- Director and hardware risk originally shared a correlated PRNG position.
- Ambiguous item insertion and XP exceptions needed committed receipts first.
- A short payoff could precede asynchronous texture readiness.
- Repair duration was initially changed after native queue creation captured it.
- The warning scan read combat state before rejecting attenuated distant floors.

One overlapping pair of test runners conflicted in their shared installer
sandbox. Those results were discarded and the complete gate was rerun alone.
Early tower fixtures let the operator bleed out before quiet speech/repair
checks; population testing and quiet scenarios were separated. Millisecond-
quantized timing was replaced with the existing high-resolution bridge clock.

New cues exercised native playback paths; human listening was not recorded as

record does not claim a controlled zombie-attraction experiment.

Debug trial switch
-------------------
In a debug-enabled game, set SCConfig.debugLockKind="word" or "remember"
before opening the next newly settled locked computer. The override is consumed
once, accepts only implemented kinds, and does not unlock an unpowered PC or
remove physical prerequisites. The release default remains nil. Existing saved
settled locks are not rerolled by this switch.
