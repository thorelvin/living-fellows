# 0.18.1 Social Contract Hardening

This patch makes the 0.18 social-contract loop observable, transactional, and safe across save/load.

## Player-facing changes

- Every accepted contract owns one unique `[LF Contract]` world-map annotation. It is synchronized without duplicates and removed when the contract completes or closes.
- Supply and medical contracts show each requirement as available, required, and missing in the player's real nested inventory.
- Medical help accepts clean bandages, alcohol bandages, or clean ripped sheets; disinfectant or alcohol wipes satisfy the disinfectant requirement. Dirty rags are deliberately excluded.
- Delivery moves the selected real items from the player to the representative through the existing ownership-checked, capacity-checked, rollback-safe trade transaction.
- Local-threat completion requires the kill target, no remaining loaded threats, and at least 64 loaded squares in the reported area. A partially loaded edge can no longer report a false completion.
- Acceptance, changing delivery readiness, confirmed kills, twelve-hour and three-hour deadline thresholds, completion, withdrawal, expiry, and no-blame closures create bounded persistent notifications.
- Withdrawing a promise from the UI requires a second click within eight seconds.
- The Factions panel shows reserve quantities and policy refusal reasons before trade selection.
- House access, lowered-weapon enforcement, trespass behavior, and safe-rest readiness are reported separately.

## Lifecycle and persistence

The social sub-schema migrates additively to version 2. Contract markers, receipts, progress, notification history, notification de-duplication, promises, and deadlines are bounded and serializable. If a medical patient dies, that contract closes without increasing the player's broken-promise count. If every household member dies, all offers, active contracts, private contacts, and guest access close cleanly.

## Verification

Deterministic gameplay coverage exercises all three contract types across a mid-contract export/restore, alternative-goods delivery, marker add/remove isolation, deadline notifications, safe-rest boundaries, reserve reasons, patient death, and household destruction. UI contract tests cover progress, remaining time, markers, notifications, reserves, safe-rest status, English translations, scrolling, and withdrawal confirmation. The protected live harness validates the Build 42 item identities, real inventory preview, map bridge, bounded notification state, and reserve APIs inside a cloned save.
