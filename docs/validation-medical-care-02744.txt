Implemented Phase 3 behavior
  Deep wounds without glass can be stitched when both actors are semi-safe.
  Undressed bleeding comes first; glass removal and disinfection precede
  stitching. One claimed helper/patient treatment reserves an exact fresh
  dressing, uncovers the part, verifies native stitching, settles the exact
  suture/thread debit once, and redresses. Reusable needles and holders remain.
  Native skill, duration, pain, stitch time, infection roll and XP rules apply.
  Ready stitches continue through the existing safe removal/aftercare path.

  Camp medics use existing visiting-patient jobs and holds. Away from camp,
  actual Decision updates can discover a nearby free allied helper whose
  First Aid is at least two levels higher, including when the patient has no
  own kit. Claims/holds release on danger, orders, group changes and urgent
  bleeding; no independent medicine owner or fabricated body-part index exists.

  Painkillers require Pain 2 or higher and no pending/working native dose.
  painEffect, painDelta and food analgesia must all be clear. A proven expired
  negative pain timer is accepted only after the other effects clear. A patient
  gets at most four doses per game day; durable allowances never refill on
  reload or an uncertain native outcome. Painful procedures can lead to this
  same medicine check once the companion is safe and otherwise qualifies.

  Antibiotics require an actual local infected wound: still infected twelve
  game hours after the first verified disinfect of that episode, or proven
  absence of permitted disinfectant. Knox alone never qualifies; an active
  antibiotic course blocks another. Fewer than two usable camp doses reserves
  treatment for the worst eligible local infection. Native eat applies and
  consumes the helper-carried dose; LF never performs a second medicine debit.
  Normal food selection excludes medicine, and the player receives no medicine.

  Camp supply observations honor exact identities, withdrawals, reserves,
  work permissions and protected stock. Bounded partial/unreadable scans remain
  unknown. Commit rescans exact contents, including same-count replacements,
  before relying on no-disinfectant or scarce-stock eligibility.
  Favorite, personal, manual, held, active-needs and foreign-loan resources are
  refused at selection and commit. Only a validated own medic-kit ancestor
  loan is permitted; a loan-marked dose or another loan marker remains refused.

  Medics keep a physical kit, two usable clean dressings, painkillers, a suture
  needle or reusable needle plus thread, and seek available tweezers. Sewing
  supplies can come from Crafting/Tools without changing storage categories.
  Optional shortages cannot starve later optional supplies; a newly depleted
  required resource reopens stocking during the optional-search cooldown.
  Exact durable receipts quarantine ambiguous effects/debits instead of
  replaying, refunding or creating items. Basic wound care and movement remain
  available. Optional care metadata stays bounded at 512 values/depth four.
  No sleeping/mood medicines or zombie navigation/pathing changes were added.

Native surgery XP and persistence
  The shared surgical XP path now uses the public native six-argument AddXP
  overload. It preserves each vanilla base request and ordinary learning
  multipliers without making a companion a local player or adding an XP ledger.
  Stitching conditionally requests base 15 First Aid XP once; actual earned XP
  depends on native profession, trait, book and sandbox multipliers.
  Saved skills resolve canonical IDs via Perks.FromString, not display names;
  unknown/sentinel/mismatched IDs are refused. Positive XP before the first
  PerkInfo/level is captured from the public registry, deduplicated within 128
  records. Restore verifies the native learned level, retains passive floors,
  then restores the exact XP remainder with all multipliers disabled.
