distinguish an unloaded car from a missing/destroyed car before abandoning it.
Source anchors: SCVehicle.lua:1362; SCExpeditionCar.lua:1462,1506,1854;
SCExpeditionPrototype.lua:4026. Removing only the outer wood condition is insufficient.
