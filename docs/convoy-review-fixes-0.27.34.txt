Convoy review fixes - 0.27.34
2026-11-17

Changes
1. Startup exit: Decision records the observed lead and immediately binds it
   through the native bridge when it starts the follower. A player exit before
   the first periodic convoy pulse now enters the normal bounded parking attempt.
   A new assignment still waits if the player has never started driving.
   Switching lead cars binds the new identity immediately and starts a fresh
   follower progress clock; a restarted wheel lease does the same.
2. Retry after refusal: an exact zero-steering, zero-speed, braking Hold retains
   the current verified lead identity. Retrying the same driverless target can
   recover. Hold clears arrival and scan facts, and a resumed route scans again.
   Manual steering, moving cruise, an unbraked stop and full release discard the
   identity; Hold cannot authorize a different unattended car or revive a release.
3. Rolling target: native parking success requires finite, stopped lead speed
   as well as the follower's existing speed and geometry checks. Lua independently
   checks both cars at the dismount boundary. Engine loss clears stale arrival.
   The existing eight-second stall and twenty-second overall parking deadlines
   still apply; a continuously rolling target cannot renew the absolute deadline.
