EMMA contextual follow-ups - 0.27.5
2026-11-08

Scope
Production offline Lua, extending the care/understanding/survival-reference slice.
No browser-demo changes, new save schema, background service or native bridge change.

SCEmmaFollowups resolves common second questions across equipment, people,
relationships, camp setup, jobs, storage, expeditions, radio, building controls,
farming and orders. Existing item-count and named-person follow-ups remain active.
Replies store subject/facet IDs and one parent subject in the existing bounded
conversation, never actor objects, readings, executable requests or permanent notes.

Read-only instructions reuse the existing guides. Current questions reuse the
existing world providers and camp-access checks. A generator fuel follow-up
reads its local power provider once; a vehicle question cannot borrow that gauge.
The latest subject wins, with its parent available for related details. Unknown
references clarify rather than reviving an older item or person. A named choice
answers a short pending clarification; a new question clears it.
