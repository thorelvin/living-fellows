  prefers service, then owner, then other cards. Stale/closed/powerless/far windows
  cannot use the callback. It also works before recruiting any companion.

  stripe checks. Tier-2 XP/payoff apply; slip recovery cannot encode a service card.
  Encoding records its receipt before modifying this owner's carried card.
