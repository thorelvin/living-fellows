# Civic desks, local firms and outbreak cards

28 October 2026, `living_fellows/0.26.74`, after `9b27e043`.

Six new transparent terminal headers cover Brooks Public Library, Louisville
Police Headquarters, Upscale Mobility, E.P. Tools, Brott Cattle Farm and
BeefChunk Jerky Co. Their names and premises were checked against the installed
42.21.0 game adverts and `PrintMediaDefinitions.MiscDetails`. Brott is a cattle
farm and livestock auction. Broad airport, mall, estate and event rectangles
remain excluded rather than lending one identity to unrelated tenants.

The catalogue now has **218 distinct identities: 202 named marks and 16 sector
or camp emblems**. The stock-map audit covers 3,536 desktop objects, 63 curated
advert keys and 62 rectangles, yielding 307 unambiguous rectangle candidates
and 29 exact room candidates. These are assignment candidates, not proof that
every computer in a running save has been visited or rebranded.

Each identity has a new four-entry staff archive: a librarian, police records
clerk, dealership bookkeeper, tool-counter worker, weighing-desk clerk and
quality-control worker. That adds **24 personal entries**, bringing the total to
**540 entries from 135 owners**. All previously published owner IDs and their
positions in the selection table are retained. Saved computers keep their
existing owner rather than acquiring a different history.

Six business reference cards and ten dated outbreak fragments bring the county
and EMMA catalogue to **136 files**, with **26 outbreak fragments**. New fragments
include *The fifth coat*, *Signature required*, *Two suppers* and *End of the reel*.
They mix small acts of care, institutional cruelty and unresolved accounts.
Staff biographies and incidents are original mod fiction; the uncertain
fragments retain the explicit **Unverified archived note** source label. None
establishes an infection cause, present occupants, supplies or a safe route.
Date gates, related-file browsing and typed access remain in place. Nothing
adds runtime networking or hosted-model access.

Brooks uses sand, Louisville Police and E.P. Tools use aqua, Upscale Mobility
and BeefChunk use sand, and Brott uses green. The library and police wordmarks
use neutral ink that follows the current phosphor, including purple. The other
marks retain pale firm colours with complementary screen pairings. All PNGs
are copied unchanged from generated output, with transparent margins and no
extra backing-removal key. The art manifest records the selected source,
prompt, identity reference, actual image inputs, hashes and alpha bounds.
Unreadable intermediate monochrome refinements were rejected before selection.

## Verification

Focused checks pass: 12,224 brain/world assertions, 6,175 security/archive
assertions, 2,001 lore/branding assertions and 3,383 knowledge assertions.
They cover the six new site identities, neighbours and private-home exclusion,
matching staff archives, colour selection, purple priority, saved-owner
retention, every new alias, original text bounds, dates and related links.
The complete UI suite passes, including keyboard access to all 136 files at
full and narrow viewport sizes. Source-byte, alpha and asset-hash checks pass
for the six additions. No generated bitmap pixels are post-processed.
The full project gate passes **29/29 stages** in 141 seconds (144 seconds total),
including normal/debug compilation of 241 Lua files, compatibility regressions,
installer transactions, Workshop and standalone packaging.

Native acceptance reopens an actual powered desktop in the disposable camp at
1280x720. All six headers, staff menus and palettes were visually inspected,
along with purple library/police variants. All five personal-file shortcuts
remain visible, including wrapped menu titles. All sixteen new reference
aliases resolve through real terminal input; archive ownership, date refusal,
reading anchors, existing item queries and the single player slot are retained.
The clone's current date exposes 133 of the 136 references; the three later
reports stay hidden. Header previews are presentation fixtures on the camp
computer; production location assignment is verified separately in headless
tests and against the stock map census.
