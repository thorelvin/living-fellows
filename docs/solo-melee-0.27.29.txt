Open-ground solo melee -- 0.27.29

Behavior

A fit, armed lone companion on verified outdoor ground keeps a local melee
exchange with a small frontal group. It backsteps to protect the weapon's
inner range, makes a bounded forward run when crowded, turns back, and lets
the nearest pursuer enter that weapon's next native swing window. Waiting
between steps prevents short blades from matching a zombie's walking speed
forever without creating an attack opening.

Admission requires a loaded, outdoor three-metre escape corridor with clear
flanks, normal floor and no stairs or trees. The allowance covers at most five
nearby upright threats clustered on the pursuer side. Health, wounds,
endurance reserve, fatigue, load, flanks, encirclement, blocked ground, human
threats and squad withdrawal retain their ordinary safety gates. Held posts
and stairs retain their existing policy. The native swing lease and physical
strike-barrier, range, collision and recovery gates remain authoritative.

Base Watch's player anchor can be the watched companion. Self identity is
excluded from support counts. An older target commitment cannot replace
verified local footwork against the nearest pursuer with full withdrawal.
Risk keeps the open lane while target selection changes or the selected
zombie falls; ordinary safe-finisher rules still own the floor attack.

Only companions move differently. Zombie navigation is untouched. Initial
spotted(companion,true) is the sole seeded detection in the live fixture.

Validation method

The earlier football field and native Base Watch view are reused. Each case
has a fresh recruited fighter, Strength/Fitness 5, melee/Maintenance 3,
standard clothing, one full-condition weapon and full starting endurance.
There are no stat refills, equipment grants or player support during combat.
Native game speed, collision, damage and zombie pursuit are retained;
multi-hit is inherited TRUE. Native damage callbacks and observed health
changes are recorded separately from contacts and deaths.

Fresh clones of the same seed are compared with unchanged 0.27.28. Native
random rolls are not synchronized. Every trial begins with a loaded-floor
cleanup of all 2,025 squares and native static-corpse readback. One isolated
human observer stays away from the fight. A recorder PASS means complete
evidence; combat outcomes have separate acceptance.

Problem cases: crowbar/3, baseball bat/3, hunting knife/3 and lead pipe/4.
Controls: crowbar and hunting knife with 0, 1 and 2 zombies. Empty fields
must remain idle; combat controls must clear without injury or withdrawal.

Production and fixture regressions

scripts/Test-OpenGroundMelee.ps1 compiles the changed production files with
the game's normal and debug Kahlua compiler. Its production-path checks
cover local risk, weapon-specific waiting and early strikes, bounded runs,
target commitment, self support, strike barriers and safety refusals.
Individual source-copy reverts of self support, overrun admission, action
scoring, forward-running gait and target commitment must each exit nonzero.
The ordinary Behaviour release check also includes the policy harness.

scripts/Test-SoloMeleeFixture.ps1 verifies native static-corpse removal and
readback, including a nonzero-exit old moving-object-cleanup negative.
Existing coordination, squad and guard-post production harnesses are run
separately. Exhaustion, injury, blocked-ground and flank negatives are
harness coverage; the healthy field trials do not establish their live
success rates.

Accepted native results

Two fresh-clone repetitions of each problem case used the exact final
SCCombat/SCMeleeKiting source, verified by SHA-256. All eight fights cleared
at 100 health with zero observed retreat time, zero overrun time and no
backward damaging contacts. The unchanged baseline had one clear, four
withdrawals and three deaths across the corresponding eight fights.

The exact final source also passed all six 0/1/2-zombie controls: both empty

Additional five-zombie stress observations on the final source: crowbar
cleared five unhurt in 28.34 s; hunting knife cleared five unhurt in 87.40 s.

repeated five-zombie acceptance or a measured safe enemy-count limit.

Kiting can use substantial ground: the longer accepted three-zombie knife
fight reached 31.82 m from its start; the five-zombie knife fight reached
29.10 m. Each run pulse is bounded, but the whole encounter is not confined
to that pulse's three-metre lane. Native stationary swings still own their
animation; the observed behavior is footwork, settle, strike, footwork.

Final release validation

Windows PowerShell 5.1 Test-Project.ps1 -Release passed all five checks in
949.3 s, including 18/18 installer transaction cases. The policy harness
passed 37 checks with five nonzero-exit reverted-fix controls. Coordination,
squad (68 checks) and guard-post (97 checks) harnesses passed. The final live
fixture compiled in normal/debug modes and passed its seven cleanup checks;
the old-cleanup negative exited 1.

The remaining six weapon types, broader repeated four/five-zombie coverage
and single-hit settings still need native coverage of this tuning. This
report establishes the recorded outdoor cases, not general survival rates.
