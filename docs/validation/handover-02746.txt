Implemented behavior
--------------------
Primary-player death pauses succession. The stock character creator can make a
fresh survivor at a valid marked camp; takeover permanently transfers an eligible
living recruit into a stock primary IsoPlayer at the recruit's location. Native
serialization preserves their body, identity, skills, equipment and needs.
Takeover retires the NPC without killing it. The previous player follows native
corpse, inventory and reanimation rules. No enemies are cleared or protection
granted by the feature. Camp arrival checks actual loaded floor, obstruction and
fire; a failed arrival retains an ordinary-spawn alternative.

Camp state, recruitment, duties and relationships remain world-owned. Separated
followers preserve their standing order while seeking reachable indoor shelter.
Contact or a changed order ends the hold. Stranded crew gets the remote view;
remote expeditions/Base Watch are saved and suspended. Settled groups can release
the slot for another cold group, with the activity resuming once rescue permits.
Radio reports require real powered equipment, channel/range compatibility and a
native receipt. Temporary endpoint capabilities are explicitly released.

A committing journal owns interrupted transfers until a read-only player-database
check confirms the ready marker. Menu preflight recovers before vanilla routes a
dead primary row to its creator. Retired IDs are excluded from synchronous and
scheduled saves, normal restoration and deliberately stale snapshots. EMMA keeps
world discoveries, marks the former operator dead and greets the successor once.
Vanilla respawn, menu and quit callbacks remain. Extra human split-screen players
are rejected; the LF-owned remote slot is supported. Zombie navigation is unchanged.

Readiness guards
----------------
Dead/unrecruited or quarantined actors are unavailable. A pending pickup inventory
transfer, expedition vehicle/resource/delivery ownership or selected carrier's
unsettled cargo gives a visible refusal. Eligible on-foot expedition members can
detach, handing leadership to a survivor or ending an empty mission. Borrowed
driver keys settle before capture. Exact native vehicle occupants release only
after the transfer receipt is durable; unrelated occupants are never evicted.

not claimed as a complete native mission/terrain matrix. Menu/quit during an
uncommitted choice is retained through stock callbacks, not a native exit matrix.
