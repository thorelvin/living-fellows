Method and quality

36 circles: three stock scripts, cruise targets 6/12/20 km/h, each with inputs
+1,-1,+0.5,-0.5. Actual steady speeds are roughly 5/11/19 km/h because the
ordinary cruise controller coasts below its target. Analysis uses actual speed.
A radius-25 open area at 6428.5,5445.5 received ordinary native asphalt floors
before measurement. Each trial provisioned a fresh car at full part/item
condition, full tyre pressure, fuel, battery and a real key. Factory part types
were retained. The companion boarded through the normal vehicle API and drove
through SC.Driving / native CarController / physics. No pose, velocity, repair
or stat writes occurred during measurement. No zombie navigation, targeting,
population, movement or state overrides were used. Background LF decisions and
arrivals were isolated in the disposable fixture.

The recorder rejects body damage, driver injury, zombie contact or leaving
the pad. Ordinary native tyre/suspension/brake/muffler wear is recorded rather
than repaired. Part-condition/pressure/friction traces distinguish it from a
collision. One false recorder wear rejection and one natural zombie-contact
attempt were discarded and repeated. Initial exploratory grass data is retained
outside the accepted asphalt matrix.

Steering mapping

The requested input reaches the wheels as an angle in radians, with reversed
native sign: positive input produces negative CarController.vehicleSteering
and a clockwise world turn. At these speeds the clamp is:
  clamp(v) = 0.3 + 0.6 * (1 - min(1, abs(v) / script.maxSpeed)).

For abs(input)>0.1 the engine eases toward the UNCLAMPED negative input, then
clamps. Per update the factor is 0.06 * (GameTime.multiplier/0.8) *
max(0.1,1-speed/maxSpeed). For abs(input)<=0.1 it centres at 0.04 rad times
that time factor, with a 0.04-rad snap. A simulator that eases toward the
already-clamped angle or treats all small inputs proportionally is inaccurate.

Initial measured exponential time constants are
0.2702..0.2807s.
Nine additional unclipped moving response fits after completed circles give
0.2823..0.3743s,
matching the speed/maxSpeed factor. Detailed fits are in steering-response.csv.
Clipped full-input tails are excluded rather than treated as exponential data.

The simple R=L/tan(delta) formula misses the vehicle-origin circle by up to
29.77% and still misses the reconstructed rear-axle circle by
24.18%. It fails the plan's 15% condition. The body origin is
ahead of the rear axle; native tyre slip adds a further difference.

Re-derived body-origin radius model, with v=actual_kmh/3.6 and delta the actual
steady controller/wheel angle:
  R_body = L/tan(abs(delta)) * (a0 + a1*tan(delta)^2 + a2*v^2).
Base.CarNormal: a0=0.940947862, a1=0.249484735, a2=0.003391828
Base.Van: a0=0.932745624, a1=0.265695493, a2=0.004024477
Base.PickUpTruck: a0=0.938321331, a1=0.246610224, a2=0.003254757

Coefficients are fitted using the six positive-input cases per car and checked
against the six negative-input cases: worst error 4.07%. Fitting on two cruise
targets and checking the omitted target gives worst error 12.22%. Both meet the
15% Phase 0 tolerance. This is an empirical model of this measured asphalt /
roadworthy-car domain, not a universal tyre model. Inputs below 0.5, higher
speeds, damaged/missing tyres, rain/snow and off-road remain uncalibrated.
The model also fits the body-origin velocity direction from the measured
instantaneous centre of rotation; see mapping.json and the scratch model.

among 2,252 internal vertices, with a maximum same-sign small-bend run of two.

sequence of tiny (<12-degree) vertices. Other maps are not covered by this scan.
