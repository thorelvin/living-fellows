Confirmed destination problems and policy
-----------------------------------------
The native catalogue was instantiated in a disposable game using getAllItems
and instanceItem. Item class and display category differ: some medical supplies
such as antibiotics are Food, scalpels and tire irons are Weapons, and many raw
components are Material.
Filename fragments also caused unrelated round furniture and BackgammonBoard
to be classified as ammunition. General milk-container capability incorrectly
made ordinary canteens and some keepsakes farm supplies.

Camp classification now uses specific native purpose before general class/name
fallbacks. Medical kits, instruments and pills go to Medical; cooking, fishing,
vehicle work, communication and lighting equipment go to Tools; maps go to
Literature; water vessels go to Water; reusable bags go to Clothes; weapon
parts go to Weapons. Native ammunition cases and fast-reload bags remain with
Ammunition. A real plunger spear remains a weapon. Dedicated farming equipment
and manure retain their existing shelves.

Usable building supplies go to Construction; other raw components go to
Crafting. The explicit building set and native tags include timber, nails,
wire, sheet metal, fired brick/tile/shingle, stone blocks, concrete, plaster
and wallpaper supplies. Unfired clay pieces, ingots, leather, thread, screws
and other crafting components remain Crafting. Filled pure cow/sheep milk is
Fresh food; an empty drinking vessel keeps its ordinary Water purpose.

Logistics deposits now use the same certain storage classification as camp
sorting, floor intake and car unloading. Combat/loadout roles remain separate:
a hammer still satisfies a weapon role while its preferred shelf is Tools.
The one intended loadout-role correction is Base.Garbagebag: a usable native
inventory container is carry gear, not loose trash selected by its filename.

Existing valid secondary placement is retained for dual-use hand tools on
Tools or Weapons. Builders and farmers can find eligible tools on Weapons
using the ordinary withdrawal, reserve and loan guards. Milking can borrow
eligible vessels from Farming, Water, Tools or Crafting; both urgent staging
and later release validate the exact original source and receipt. Valuables,
memorial and General are not new automatic milk supply sources. Animal-care
cleaner lookup also includes Tools, where bleach already sorts.

Unknown mod items retain uncertain classification and manual dedicated-shelf
placement. General source remains unsorted intake. Ordinary sorting empties it
into dedicated shelves; car unloading retains its existing explicit staging
fallback. Capacity, deposit permissions, reserves, personal ownership and work
receipts continue to gate actual transfers. No item definitions or zombie
navigation are changed.
