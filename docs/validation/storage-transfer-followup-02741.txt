Confirmed defect and correction
------------------------------
An ordinary logistics deposit selected a marked container but did not retain
its storage row. During walking or the native Loot pose, unmarking/relabeling
that shelf could leave the cached container selected. Only loadout-preparation
deposits ran storage permission validation at commit; ordinary deposits checked
capacity and still transferred into the unmarked or newly Medical container.

Both ordinary and preparation deposits now retain the exact selected storage
row, container and category. Route candidates also keep the category snapshot.
The shared check validates registration identity, current container, deposit
permission and unchanged category before approach and immediately before commit.
Native transfer completion and preparation inventory reconciliation still precede
the final check. Failure uses the existing transaction cleanup: the exact carried
item and return markers remain with the companion, native ownership is released,
and ordinary later selection/retry may choose a valid shelf. Existing optional
preparation deadlines keep their precedence. General/Output staging fallbacks
remain valid because validation compares the selected category, rather than
requiring every fallback to equal the preferred item category.

The correction does not alter packing, dropping, withdrawing or zombie navigation.
The companion's real native inventory transfer remains the only commit operation.
No version or bridge-protocol bump is introduced for this requested 41 follow-up.
