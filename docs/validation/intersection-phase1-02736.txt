Implementation
--------------
SCDrivePath.withCorners creates bounded, checked lane arcs or retains original
sharp corners, with stable IDs, reasons, original-route distance mapping and
unchanged source points outside corner spans. Per-leg widths work in both
directions, including street/driveway transitions. The builder keeps the source
route, respects endpoint reserves and the 1,024-point budget, and never reparses
the denser generated geometry.

Only SCCompanionDriver.follow opts in. Convoy following, approach and parking
retain their original paths and command streams. No router, route serialization,
saved data, walking, Lua gameplay or zombie navigation code changed.

The follower uses each complete corner's angle, holds its speed cap until the
physical heading aligns, blends curvature steering only around arcs, and rebuilds
the original route on a return trip. Native wheel offsets are used as-is, without
modelScale. Body-origin velocity direction is observed from motion, avoiding
production dependence on the empirical Phase 0 tyre coefficients. Look-ahead and
scan distances account for the difference between the encoded centreline and the
physical lane arc. Approach braking uses the existing conservative deceleration
and margins, avoiding a crawl over the entire stopping distance.

An established sideways offset stays fixed on an arc. Limited passing lines are
tried before entry; a late change without a pursuit span plus body length of
straight room falls back to the original corner. A blocked corner switches back
at most once. Scans merge adjacent small arc pieces, include sagitta and body
sweep, and skip sub-millimetre clipped fragments rather than classifying their
zero-length heading as an unloaded obstacle. nextVertexAlong is binary.

driveStatus adds corners, cornersSharp, cornerFallbacks, laneError and maxLaneError.
Lane error is omitted while passing, reversing or not following normally.

Plan clarifications
-------------------
1. V2's literal extended incoming half-plane test rejects legal large left turns
   after they enter the exit road. A point in the outgoing corridor beyond the
   junction is accepted; its exit lane must still be on the right. Roads narrower
   than six metres have no divided traffic lanes, matching the existing offset
   rule. Dedicated off-side right/left fixtures still fail V2.
2. Widths come from the leg anchors before/after the junction. The junction node
   may carry either street's width. Taking max(endpoint widths) would keep an
   eight-metre lane into a five-metre driveway and would not reverse correctly.
3. F7's cached square-query budget alone cannot detect removal of merging. A
   second work budget checks actual strip traversals. With merging it is 4 at
   the chosen arc-entry pose; without merging it is 7, exceeding the budget of 6.
   The original 2x square-query budget is retained in the wreck scenario.
