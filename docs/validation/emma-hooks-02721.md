# EMMA reply hooks, Phase B

`SCEmmaHooks` derives bounded public-reply metadata once from EMMA's language
pools, dated local archive and all three persona rule/dark pools. Salient terms
include a curated noun list and capitals within sentences. Archive aliases are
explicit hooks on their own entries. Known terms have short explanations;
other terms stay attached to their actual authored subject and honestly stop
at the record's limits. The lint prints uncovered terms and accepts an explicit
`closed` declaration. Tests remove a real hook and add an uncovered term.

Fragments use the last public reply's metadata. Complete verb-bearing questions
keep their own subject. Existing direct guides and persona commands outrank a
generic hook on a menu label. Guide continuations retain their subject; public
miss receipts now include the hook names. Private notes, operator names, care
and conversation history never become public hook material. Session role
claims and greetings containing a claimed role are private for logging/export.

Archive facets distinguish the record's date/source from the event's date or
author. Missing causes, identities and present locations remain unknown.
Fixed geography is checked against the installed 42.22 English challenge,
radio and map descriptions. Knox County appears in old local files; the wider
modern-map region is called Knox Country. No current safety or distance is
inferred from that background.

Governor, president, head of state, soldier, doctor, police, scientist and Knox
Telecommunications claims get specific replies. Unknown article-led role nouns
get the generic credentials reply. Claims stay in the session; they grant no
lock access or executable action. SPIFFY, Ellis and Duty answer in their own
voices, with Duty requesting authorisation codes. SPIFFY has explicit answers
for Kyle and the Smile Score. First-person conditions use practical help after
the existing care/self-worth checks.

Weather selection uses declared sky/season tags and, when needed, cloud,
temperature and daylight bounds. A missing cloud reading cannot establish a
clear sky. The provider uses the native `getCloudIntensity` getter also used by
Build 42's foraging UI. Unverified claims about wind/footstep masking, snow
tracks and heat slowing zombies are retired. The negative control removes the
actual selector filter and demonstrates false selections.

The older corpus now recognises practical condition guides, session-role
replies and dated lore facets as semantic equivalents to its former generic
chat/lore labels. Their content has focused assertions. Care equivalence and
detection rules are unchanged. The original exact-input change list and the
original/Phase A passing-row floor remain enforced.
