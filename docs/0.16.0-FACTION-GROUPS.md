# 0.16.0 faction and group architecture

Living Fellows separates two concepts:

- Player `Groups` remain command and formation assignments for recruited companions.
- World `Factions` own autonomous residents, territory, standing, needs, trade, offenses, and persistence.

The first archetype is `barricaded_household`. A production household contains one to three permanent native companions, is limited to three active households per save, begins no earlier than day seven, and observes a seven-day successful-spawn cooldown plus a rare daily roll. Houses must be loaded, previously unseen for production, unburned, outside the player's current building and Living Fellows base, outside multiplayer safehouses, and at least 300 tiles from another living household.

Each household preserves its bounds, interior spawn points, exterior openings, primary entry, members, fortification jobs, standing, offenses, shortage, request, reserved reward, and history. Active actors are stored by `SCPersistence.factionActors`; hibernated actor snapshots live in the faction document. Streaming uses a 120-tile hibernation and 100-tile wake band, fresh square resolution, spawn queue claims, and persistence-pending checks to prevent duplicates.

Residents begin Wary. They discover and warn at roughly 18 and 10 tiles, record sustained trespass, react to aiming, theft, damage, barricade interference, and murder, and remain inside a bounded pursuit leash. Murder creates permanent hostility. Other offenses require three or seven days of cooling-off and a full restitution parcel; theft and injury require twice their standing damage. Item movement validates current ownership, nested containers, capacity, protected gear, and every committed transfer. A failed transaction rolls back.

Fortification is a real gameplay activity. Secondary exterior doors and windows receive a two-plank first pass and four-plank final pass through native timed actions and actual materials. The primary entry remains usable unless sustained danger creates an emergency seal, which is later reopened. Threats pre-empt construction, and members close doors and curtains while guarding.

Every household is generated with one meaningful shortage and a matching request. The leader holds a reserved reward that ordinary barter cannot expose. Completion adds 30 standing and unlocks barter. Tolerated households require 125 percent offered value; Trusted households trade at equal value. Household survival reserves, equipped gear, personal items, nonempty bags, and unfinished fortification materials are excluded from stock.

The translucent Factions tab shows only discovered households. The Debug tab exists only when `debugSpawnEnabled` is true and provides explicit 1/2/3/random household spawning, inspection, standing, request/trade, job advancement, and confirmed deletion of debug-created groups. Runtime no longer schedules one-minute debug spawning.
