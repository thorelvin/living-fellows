Convoy following and parking - 0.27.33
2026-11-17

Behaviour
The moving follower now aims for four metres of body clearance plus 0.2 seconds
of lead-car speed, with closing-speed braking and a 50 km/h controller ceiling.
This is a target, not a guaranteed fixed separation during acceleration or turns.
The rolling trail no longer applies destination braking, and its lead car is
excluded from overtaking decisions. The physical bumper scan still sees that
car. Only an exactly identified, aligned moving lead uses relative-speed braking;
other vehicles, people, stationary obstacles and unloaded ground keep their
normal checks. The obstacle's vehicle identity is cached with its scan, because
a moving lead can leave the sampled square before the next frame.

When the player exits, the follower retains the remembered lead car and its
wheel owner. A final approach at up to 5 km/h targets two metres between the
actual native hulls, behind the lead car's rear, accounting for body size and
orientation. A native parking confirmation requires 1.5--2.8 metres of body
clearance, lateral offset <=0.7 metres, matching headings, and speed <=0.6 km/h.
The driver then releases the wheel and uses normal safe vehicle exit. Overflow
passengers retain their seats through the approach and intermediate stops.
Only the same previously followed car can become a driverless parking target.

Parking is bounded by eight seconds without 0.5 metres of real movement, or
twenty seconds overall. Movement cannot renew the absolute deadline. Failure
stops the car and attempts its normal exit, with a specific unreachable-parking
message. A rolling car, rejected native stop or blocked door cannot be replaced
with a forced exit. The selected follow car is retained for a later drive, and
ordinary foot Follow resumes after exiting. Player reboarding resets the parking
attempt; survival preemption keeps its existing priority.
A restored seated follower without a remembered lead uses the same bounded
fallback. A newly assigned car still waits for the player to begin driving.
