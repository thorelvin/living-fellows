# 0.14.7 awareness and follow routing

This release adds three bounded behaviors to the existing movement system.

## Rear awareness

While a companion is settled in its assigned follow slot and no danger is active,
a stable phased timer can request a brief look behind the established travel
heading. The companion then restores forward formation facing. The action is sent
only to the validated companion actor; it never drives the local player.

## Room entry

Crossing from the current room into a different indoor room creates a single
threshold sequence: stop and observe, sweep left from the approach heading, sweep
right, then advance. Urgent movement skips the sequence. The sequence is keyed to
the exact source/destination edge so ordinary updates cannot restart it every frame.

## Follow route evaluation

Longer follow, formation, and regroup paths may compare up to three distinct routes.
The primary search retains its normal bounded budget. Alternative searches use a
smaller 80-node budget, rotated neighbor order, and penalties on already-used path
segments. Selection scores real traversal cost, nearby threat exposure, actor
crowding, and excessive turns. Other jobs and urgent survival routes retain their
existing behavior.

## Live acceptance checks

1. Hold formation in a safe area until the companion looks behind, then forward.
2. Walk through several indoor doorways and verify one left/right sweep per entry.
3. Add visible danger during a check and verify survival movement takes priority.
4. Follow around an obstacle with two viable routes and watch for stable progress,
   no doorway loop, and no rapid route oscillation.
5. Confirm every shout, facing change, crouch, and movement belongs to the companion.
