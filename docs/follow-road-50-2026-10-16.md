The three fixes are: release a stale trail hold once the leader is moving beyond catch-up range; allow running-squad medical rescues only when helper and patient are both beside the moving player with an open short route; and cancel an active approach from both the medical update and direct treatment delegate when the casualty is left behind. Failed approaches receive a short retry cooldown. The gameplay suite passed, including 81 medical liveness checks and 920 static gameplay assertions.

Remaining issue: Judith spent 13 of the 23 run samples in `path_searching`, compared with five in the baseline. Her peak gap improved substantially, but follow-route acquisition still deserves a separate investigation. The test is a single cloned-save run without zombies; it demonstrates this route and does not establish behavior on every terrain or squad makeup.
