Living Fellows — withdraw from combat to board the player's car
2026-11-01; working changes on living_fellows/0.26.88
Runtime: Project Zomboid 42.21.0, single player.

Problem
A following companion could retain an aggressive combat target inside a
building after the player entered a car. Combat priority and its current action
ownership prevented the ordinary passenger-door approach from being selected.

Behaviour
Following/regrouping companions with Ride with player enabled treat the player's
vehicle entry as a tactical withdrawal. They release the old engagement and run
to an assigned passenger door, including when that door is less than eight tiles
away. Existing native swing ownership finishes before withdrawal; no pending
strike is force-cleared or replayed. Contact danger, encirclement and urgent
personal care retain precedence. Distant/indoor threats cannot cancel a live
boarding transaction. Moving cars still require a safe stop before boarding.

The handoff clears only the withdrawing actor's claims, route, passive aim and
published combat state. It preserves an opponent's independent target. Repeated
boarding pulses do not stop the new approach. A native aim target surviving a
cancelled Lua owner is also reconciled. Exact seat assignments, real passenger
doors and native seat verification remain required; no virtual seats or rescue
teleports are introduced. Explicit Stay, ride opt-out, expeditions and separately
assigned follow cars retain their own policies. No save migration or idle scan.
