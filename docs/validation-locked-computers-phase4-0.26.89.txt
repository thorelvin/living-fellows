UI and artwork
--------------
The dense password board now paints individual ASCII glyphs on a constant
pixel grid. Cached native font advances choose a cell wide enough for every
glyph and its extra hover stroke. Each glyph is clipped to its own cell.
Word and matching-bracket hit regions use those exact same cells. Punctuation
cannot invade a candidate's next cell. No font measurement repeats per frame.
Tiny layouts scale the board to retain complete words and memory addresses.

Adventure choices are pinned below the scrollable scene. Compact layouts
retain full option text in the reading pane and bounded numbered controls;
the smallest layouts retain ordinary scrolling. Pictures disappear when the
layout is too small. The certificate sits beside its text, without overlap.

Original built-in image_gen artwork: title, eight class portraits, four slot
illustrations, seven monsters, owner skeleton, ghost, game-over stamp and
licence, plus a dice icon. Three shared sheets and the title supply these
independent display views without duplicating texture payloads. The existing
phosphor shader supplies every CRT colour, including purple. Sources are
losslessly packaged; the display rectangles do not alter the bitmap pixels.
