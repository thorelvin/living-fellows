Implementation
- Shared side-aware window policy for planning, traversal and native submission.
- Ordinary inside latches open normally; protected locks remain protected.
- Automatic smashing requires an active attack or a reachable imminent hostile.
- Elevated crossings require supported clear landings and an onward route
  excluding the crossed window, ending at a verified stair approach where needed.
- Retained proof uses normal bounded searches, 24 expansion nodes and 16
  revalidation edges per call. No background scan or additional service.
- Two out-and-back cycles in 30 seconds, or 30 seconds without onward progress,
  hold outward travel for 120 seconds. Checked inward recovery remains available.
- Companion-only native single-use approval covers all seven climb/frame/smash
  overloads, including native pathfinding. Players and camera slots are unchanged.
- Existing combat probe freezes dead/removed zombie lifetimes rather than
  sampling population-pool replacements. Passive controls compare initial
  wounds and health; pre-existing injuries alone cannot pass them.
- Native injury, defence, armour and miss outcomes remain authoritative.
  No new production damage deductions were added.
- Save schemas remain unchanged. Matching protocol: 42.21-isocompanion-30.
