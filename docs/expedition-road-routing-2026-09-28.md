# Expedition road routing playtest

The expedition planner now lists every eligible building within 200 straight-line tiles of the selected leader, 32 per page. Dispatch rechecks the selected building by ID instead of assuming it remains on the first page. The GUI defaults to **Follow roads** and also offers **Head straight for target**.

Road travel builds a local graph from the active map's street polylines. It splits bends and geometric junctions, attaches the leader and destination to candidate segments, and reports a disconnected or incomplete map instead of inventing a link. The graph supplies street targets only. Existing Navigation still owns each loaded movement leg, including doors, windows, collision, follower cohesion, and the final building approach. Return plans a new road route from the leader's actual position. A versioned save descriptor stores no native graph; restart rebuilds the route after the leader reclaims the second view.

### T-junctions and off-road access (0.26.5)

The game's street data draws a side street to the edge of the road it joins, not to that road's centre line. Near a Riverside base, 20 of 27 street ends that meet another street stop exactly half the joined road's width short; only 7 touch. The graph therefore joins a street end to the nearest other street when the gap is at most half that street's `WorldMapStreet.getWidth()` plus 1.5 tiles (capped at 12). The joined road is split at the exact reached point so both share one node. An end that already touches another street is left to the intersection pass, and a wider gap stays disconnected.

The leader and destination may attach up to 100 tiles from a street, up from 45. Connector length is weighted three times road length in the search, so the route keeps to the streets and leaves them close to the goal. With an observed horde, a connector of any allowed length is rejected if its straight line passes within the horde radius; the former 24-tile avoidance cap is gone. Lua verifies a road entry up front only within 45 tiles, where it can be loaded; a farther entry is walked in loaded local legs like the final approach, still checked against the avoidance area. Replaying the router on the shipped `streets.xml` from (6077, 5304), a 5-tile grid of 4,980 targets within 200 tiles went from 1,838 routable (1,574 disconnected, 1,568 without a street within 45 tiles) to 4,398 routable and 582 without a street within 100 tiles.

Straight travel uses the existing loaded-leg planner up to the 200-tile target. If no outbound leg can be found for 30 seconds, the team turns home with `straight_path_unreachable`. A failed inbound path becomes a visible technical hold rather than a fabricated return.

The leader now checks fresh, visible zombie contacts during road travel. A group of more than three zombies per living squad member near the road section ahead triggers a new route request. Scattered contacts elsewhere in view do not add to that group, and one lone road contact cannot hide a larger group farther along the road. That request temporarily excludes only the observed horde area; safe portions of a long street remain available. The local entry and walking paths must also stay outside the avoidance area. If no connected road detour is available outbound, the team turns home on a newly planned avoidance-aware route. The road itinerary, avoidance area, and return intent survive a save/reload. Road Search travel no longer uses site-access permission to cut through unrelated buildings. At the final return, the leader approaches within four tiles of the player before waiting for followers to assemble.
