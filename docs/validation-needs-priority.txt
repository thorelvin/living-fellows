Companion food priority and travel pauses
Branch: living_fellows/0.26.88
Game: Project Zomboid 42.21.0

Behaviour
- Routine meals start at 75% hunger, as explicitly chosen by the user. Vanilla's
  Starving moodle begins at 70%; this scheduling threshold is intentionally
  retained rather than silently reverted. Existing per-need emergency limits
  stay intact (hunger 82%, thirst 75%).
- Followers must be on the leader's floor, close to formation, and observe a
  15-second stop before routine eating, drinking or refilling. Entering a car,
  movement or accumulating position changes by the leader reset the stop. A
  seated follower cannot reset that shared clock for companions on foot.
  Routine needs yield
  when travel resumes, using the existing exact-action/item cancellation paths.
- Autonomous expedition travel also defers routine consumption and home errands.
  A stopped expedition crew uses carried supplies. Survival overrides remain.
- Safe fresh perishables precede ordinary durable food, then emergency-only
  insects, worms and condiments. Emergency food also requires hunger emergency;
  dehydration alone never grants permission to eat it.
- Ranking uses native Food metadata. Native isFresh also returns true for many
  indefinitely shelf-stable foods, so DaysFresh/off-age is checked too. Opened
  canned corn spoils after two days and counts as perishable. Sealed/unusable
  food remains subject to the existing safe-food checks; this change adds no
  can-opening recipe or food fabrication.
- Camp searches respect marked storage, reserves, personal items and withdrawals.
  Permitted floor transit allows workers to consider other camp floors. Travelling
  followers use sources within six tiles of the leader on the same floor.
  Once admitted, the errand can finish within that radius plus two tiles for
  interaction; it still yields when the leader moves. Per-need emergencies use
  actor-local supplies without the leader's six-tile scope. Expedition members
  are not measured against the remote player's position or pause clock.
- Item discovery retains independent bounded cursors per need/tier. Reserved
  containers and unfinished slices are pending, not proof of missing food.
  Unreachable sources receive a short retry delay so another source can be tried.
- Needs reset and interruption bookkeeping use the actual supplied decision
  runtime, avoiding stale needs values in a separate fallback runtime.

The test uses a disposable copy of LF-Skills5-Test-2026-10-29, LF alone, and the
checkout's isolated native bridge. It places one test companion by real permitted
storage, seeds real Apple/Crackers/Maggots/Ketchup items, then executes native loot
and eat actions. Player placement is a fixture; companions are not forcibly fed
or moved after initial spawning. Interrupted food is never scripted as consumed.

Coverage limits
Cross-floor selection and transit admission are automated checks. This native
scenario exercises one floor; it does not certify every multi-storey camp route.
Earlier native attempts corrected fixture assumptions: saved residents were on a
different floor, opened corn was perishable, native placement was asynchronous,
and direct test calls initially used a fallback rather than the decision runtime.
The explicit-runtime reset issue discovered while fixing that fixture is covered
by its own production regression.
