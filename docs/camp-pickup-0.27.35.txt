2026-11-17

Behavior
An equipped two-way radio or EMMA's Radio desk opens one pickup sheet. It
suggests an available camp resident, an actual parked car and a roadside stop;
the player can override the driver and car. Calls require a real request/reply.
Both endpoints can be a handset or radio-equipped computer. A camp computer
can relay locally to a resident who has no handset. Lost contact leaves the
accepted order intact. Local instructions beside the driver need no radio.
Pickup updates continue their bounded road search after one click, check fuel
and a route back to camp, then apply only after the driver's reply. Newer orders
invalidate stale callbacks.

Nearby eligible Follow/Regroup companions within 35 metres horizontally receive
ordinary reserved passenger seats. Ride opt-outs and separate-car assignments
are respected. After boarding, choose Home or a map destination. The driver
waits for ordinary exits, then returns the empty car to the original camp and
resumes duty. Deliberate parked waits survive reload. Technical loading,
stalled movement and parking waits are bounded, with explicit Retry.

The service reserves its driver, car, seats and remote-view capacity for the
whole job, including the full-primary-view passenger ride. Remote legs borrow
the existing companion view. Base Watch pauses and resumes its original or an
eligible resident through guarded native living handoffs. Manual orders release
pickup authority; urgent survival or medical work stops the journey first.

Persistence uses a separate schema-1 pickup journal and a typed native arrival
adapter. It retains the exact request, driver, native player slot and vehicle
save receipt without weakening legacy Home verification. A frozen arrival can
finish after its actor leaves Registry and its car unloads. Scoped cold restore
requires the pickup's owned secondary map. Returned pickup records retain exact
native item IDs through repeated lookup/cancellation/restoration. Ordinary Home
post-delivery inventory behavior remains covered by the original regression.
