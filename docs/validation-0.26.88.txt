Validation date: 2026-11-01

Scope

1. Prevent the Lighting64.dll+0x12b04 fault reproduced during repeated
   companion-view openings in 0.26.84 and 0.26.87. Native secondary lighting
   allocation and retirement quiesce the lighting worker before changing its
   state. Primary lighting/world caches remain intact. Timeout, interruption
   and failed native transaction paths refuse unsafe mutation and restore the
   worker where appropriate.

2. Keep active expedition routes out of routine camp logistics. Instrumented
   native play showed the leader alternating between mission movement, home
   storage surveys and supply errands. Overload/old field-return cleanup could
   outrank travel; repeated mission stall recovery then erased the other route.
   Mission cargo retains its dedicated controller. A pre-existing inventory
   transfer is cancelled at approach or finished as the same exact transaction.
   Carried food/water remain usable. Emergency needs and bathroom breaks retain
   priority, and a fresh survival route keeps its frontier through mission stall
   checks. A stale survival decision cannot hold the route indefinitely.

3. Retire known UI panels bound to released LF actors: SimpleStatus SSBar and
   vanilla ISDesignationZonePanel. Cleanup preserves the real player, an active
   secondary player and unrelated mod windows. It detaches exact stale vanilla
   aliases and guards retained status-bar hotkey callbacks. It runs only at view
   lifecycle boundaries; third-party source files are untouched.

Limits

The walking probes deliberately used a short 0.25-game-hour turn-home deadline.
They verify travel, task ownership, return and UI cleanup; they do not certify
collecting a full eight-item quota. Emergency-care routing is covered by focused
automated checks, not a separately staged live emergency. Runs used the private
SCLauncher/nosteam harness; the tester's exact Rosewood save, all 88 enabled mods
and public ZombieBuddy loader were not reproduced. The original tester's own
fatal stack was not supplied, although the native fault was reproduced on their
reported LF version in the earlier comparison.

Release operations

Lua and the rebuilt matching native bridge are shipped together. Existing save
schemas and bridge protocol are retained. Local installation and publishing the
Workshop/Field Guide are outside this commit-and-push request.
