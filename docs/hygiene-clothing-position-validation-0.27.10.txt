Behaviour
Toilet use removes lower-body clothing through stock unequip actions before
either sitting or standing. Stock wear actions restore the exact original
pants, underwear and slots before the bathroom break completes. Shirts,
shoes and bags stay worn. One shared temporary-outfit helper also handles
the existing shower outfit, avoiding a second clothing implementation.

Interruption keeps the outfit receipt until owned native actions release and
available clothing is restored. Replaced, missing and broken items remain
respected; no item is recreated. Save capture references temporarily removed
garments already present in the actor's real inventory. Clothing failures
use the existing 30-second bathroom retry delay rather than immediate loops.

Showers now require the companion to walk onto the actual shower tile before
undressing and washing, instead of using generic adjacent plumbing access.
The native Deluxe shower tile was walkable at 6547,5451,0; its old generic
approach was outside at 6548,5452,0. Entry respects static collision, normal
navigation and the equipment-action cooldown. The native wash instance faces
the shower head. There is no teleport, collision override or bridge change.
