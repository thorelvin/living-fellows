Solo firearms -- 0.27.19
2026-11-14 | Project Zomboid 42.22.2

All 100 requested live trials are recorded: ten representative vanilla guns,
one companion against 1 through 10 zombies, Aiming 3 and Reloading 3.
Result: 28 cleared, 67 timed out, 5 observed withdrawals, no companion deaths
within the measured windows. There were 195 kills out of 550 starting zombies.
This is a completed screening matrix, not a pass for firearm effectiveness.
Surviving and clearing the wave are separate results.

Implemented behaviour
  Gunners retain a viable firing target instead of switching merely because
  another zombie is closer. Clearly more wounded viable targets can regain
  priority after knockdown. Shared, throttled health observations support this
  without adding a camp service. Immediate defence, rescue, explicit player
  designation, obstruction, range, weapon changes and withdrawal still apply.

  Kiting uses the closest visible upright threat, independently of the firing
  target. Loaded guns use an aimed backward walk. Before native gun service,
  the actor makes room and stops its prior running input. An exact active
  reload/rack action may then coexist with walking when vanilla permits it.
  Stale receipts, another actor's action and running remain excluded.

  Reload/rack ownership survives distant sightings. Contact, urgent medical
  danger and explicit withdrawal can still interrupt. Completed shot input
  and completed-rack selectors no longer block the next reload. Native action
  animations, timing, sounds and ammunition transfers remain authoritative.

  Shooting utility no longer rewards distance. Provisional doctrine proposals
  no longer renew the incumbent target's commitment timer when that proposal
  is rejected. Only an accepting lane commits the actual target.

  The native candidate also registers the actual selected in-range body and
  supplies this companion's camera/body-intersection query. It does not alter
  global mouse input, player slots, hit rolls, health or damage. This remains
  a candidate aiming repair; these results do not establish an accuracy gain.

  No zombie paths, waypoints, movement flags, speeds or navigation states
  were changed. Only normal initial spotted(target) selects the LF companion.

What remains unresolved
  Shotguns performed substantially better in this screening. The two bolt
  actions cleared just one of twenty waves. Several one-zombie trials timed
  out, so spreading damage or target switching cannot explain all the misses.

  Example: MSR700 versus one used 27 rounds at a sampled selected distance
  averaging 11.52 tiles, with zero target switches. It produced 23 OnHitZombie
  callbacks but one observed accepted-damage event and 0.17 observed health
  loss. Thus collision callbacks must not be reported as damaging hits.
  Native chance, aiming pose/steadiness and engagement distance need further
  investigation before calling the gun range calibrated or rifles reliable.
  The internal spacing scale 0.7 is a tested candidate, not a proven optimum.

  OnHitZombie fires before ignore-damage handling. OnWeaponHitXp observations
  identify one accepted native damage path, but not all shotgun/range-falloff
  paths emit it. Native critical deaths can retain positive aggregate health.
  The CSV records death explicitly; neither callback counts nor kills/rounds
  are a direct hit-accuracy percentage. Racked/ejected rounds can affect the
  ammunition delta. Gunshot counters verify native emitter calls, not listening.

Method and limits
  LF alone; isolated source bridge; LowImpact; engine sound enabled. One game
  at a time, with no compiler JVM beside it. Original save and ordinary mod
  installation are untouched. The test games have exited.
  Seed: C:/Users/thore/Zomboid/Saves/Rising/2026-11-13_15-25-26.
  Requested pin: 6444,5461. Open range: 6428.5,5449.5, floor 0.
  Player: 6428.5,5509.5, initially 60 tiles away; measured minimum 51.51.
  Base Watch owns slot 1. Ownership is released between trials.

  A fresh companion per trial: Aiming/Reloading 3, Strength/Fitness 5,
  consistent background/personality, real T-shirt, jeans and trainers,
  no headgear, native vision modifier 1.0. Each fight starts at noon.
  Initial needs/endurance are set before the fight only. No mid-fight healing,
  stat resets, ammo grants, synthetic animation events or scripted damage.
  Real schoolbag, 120 reserve cartridges in loose rounds/compatible magazines,
  plus a normally loaded gun. Physical items count individually.

  Ambient zombies are cleared before each wave. Enemies spawn 12/14 tiles
  along the selected open corridor; they retain native behaviour thereafter.
  Stay is the defensive order: this is not a pursuit test. Native sight loss,
  wandering and targets leaving engagement range can cause a timeout.
  The initial open footprint extends 24 tiles; fences/buildings remain beyond.
  A world clone is shared by each batch of 50, with fresh actors and local
  corpse cleanup between trials. World conditions outside that cleanup remain.
  One trial per cell, native randomness: no statistical claim of optimal range.
  These are representative guns, not a usage ranking.

  WIN means all measured enemies died. TIMEOUT means the 90-second limit.
  WITHDREW requires displacement plus observed retreat decisions; it does not
  prove a successful escape. DEAD means native death. A fixture PASS means the
  trial was recorded correctly, not that its combat outcome was successful.
  Non-wins, stalls and first walking-service observations have zoomed-out
  native screenshots. Full raw logs and screenshots remain in the run folders.
