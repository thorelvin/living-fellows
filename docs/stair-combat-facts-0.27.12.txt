Stair combat facts -- horde plan S0 -- 2026-11-13

Measured on 0.27.12 + horde/phase-0 with the new live probe:
  scripts/Invoke-LiveSandboxTests.ps1 -StairCombatProbe -StairCombatTrials 3
      -LivingFellowsOnly -IsolatedSourceBridge -TimeoutSeconds 900
Disposable cloned save, isolated source bridge; the installed game, launcher
and saves were not changed. One run at a time.

Fixture
One north-facing staircase near 5850,5232: exit 5810,5234 z0, pieces
stairsBN 5810,5233 / stairsMN 5810,5232 / stairsTN 5810,5231, landing
5810,5230 z1. One native companion (Base.Crowbar, endurance 1, Stay order)
and one zombie targeted at it with the native spotted() path. Three trials
with the companion on the landing ("top") and three at the exit ("bottom").
Per-second timelines are printed as SC_REAL_SANDBOX|STAIR_TIMELINE lines.

Probe trap found on the way: both ends of a staircase usually fit the
"free tile, three stair tiles, free tile one floor up" shape. The probe (and
SCStairTransition's survey, which uses the same shape) can pick the floor above
the stairs' foot as the "landing". The probe now requires the bottom piece
(stairs*B*) beside the exit and the top piece (stairs*T*) beside the landing.
A north-facing staircase climbs toward -y.

  top 1   zombie climbed in 7.7 s; companion reacted at height 0.98 (top
          step); its attack staggered the zombie from 0.98 to 0.62 and floored

  bottom 3  reaction at zombie height 0.92 (top step); companion hit from flat

No companion took damage in six trials; no bite or grab landed from the stairs.

What this settles for the plan (plan-horde-combat-wp2-6.md, 4.6)
  1. Height decides who reacts: a zombie climbing to a group upstairs is not
     a threat until its height reaches about 0.97. Rule 1 is needed.
  2. Holding the top works: a hit or shove at the top sends the zombie back
     down the stairs and usually floors it.
  3. The top step can stall: a zombie standing on it beside the defender was
     neither hit nor hitting for 21 s. Holding the top needs a fallback: step
     back one tile to draw it onto the landing.
  4. Stay did not keep a defender off the stairs. Rule 2 is needed.
  5. A zombie floored on the stairs is not finished; the grounded-finisher
     logic needs a stair case (finish it or hold, with a deadline).
  6. Meeting a descending zombie from flat ground at the foot works.
Limits: one staircase, one weapon, one zombie, healthy fighter, six trials.
Climb and reaction figures are observations, not guarantees.

fight; the player can finish it.
