# 0.26.9: ten additional reliability fixes

This pass starts from 0.26.8. These are ten new failure cases, separate from
the Talk and initial chair-selection fixes in that release.

| # | Failure | Fix | Focused regression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An urgent dispatch callback returning no acceptance was recorded as dispatched. | Require explicit `true` before reporting a dispatch. | Supervisor harness: empty callback result records `failed`. |
| 2 | A cancellation callback returning no acceptance released the active owner and its reserved resource. | Retain both until cancellation is confirmed. | Supervisor harness: owner and reservation remain. |
| 3 | A second escape request for a new tile reused the old queued callback, so the actor could flee toward a stale destination. | Replace the queued target and dispatch callback while keeping its original deadline. | Supervisor harness: the new target dispatches after medical work exits. |
| 4 | A chair or other downtime resource became available after its 30-second lease even while the original activity remained active. | Treat an active owner's resource as reserved until the activity releases it. | Gameplay furniture fixture: the second actor still chooses a free chair after the lease time. |
| 5 | Vehicle manifests scanned only the first `maxCompanions` registry actors before filtering out neutrals. A recruited follower later in the registry received no seat. | Scan the complete living roster and filter eligible followers. | Core harness: twenty neutral entries precede ten recruited followers. |
| 6 | A follower with no readable world coordinates could reserve a passenger seat, ahead of a present follower when wounded. | Exclude a passenger candidate that cannot approach the car. | Core harness: positionless wounded follower receives no seat. |
| 7 | Boarding could call native vehicle entry after the action supervisor refused the commit phase. | Stop before entry and close the failed transaction. | Core harness: injected commit refusal leaves the actor on foot. |
| 8 | Boarding could remain native seated after the supervisor refused the verification phase. | Roll back native entry and keep the seat reserved only if rollback cannot be verified. | Core harness: injected verification refusal exits and releases the seat. |
| 9 | Exiting could call native vehicle exit after the supervisor refused the commit phase. | Stop before exit and close the failed transaction. | Core harness: injected commit refusal keeps the actor in its seat. |
| 10 | Exiting could remain out of the car after the supervisor refused the verification phase. | Re-enter the original seat and verify that rollback. | Core harness: injected verification refusal restores the same passenger. |

The harnesses validate the Lua transaction boundaries and native-call adapter
contract. A disposable-save playtest is still needed to confirm the visible
boarding, exit, and seating behavior in Project Zomboid.
