Reading
Three trials per case is a small sample; one bad trial (baseline 3v6 #2,
a companion killed) moves every total. What the candidate changes on
purpose is visible: phase 1 stops counting busy fighters as support, so a
fighter in its own exchange no longer gets the outer-edge allowance from
allies who are fighting too. Against six it killed fewer in the time
limit and nobody died. The user's chosen bravery (two zombies per fit
fighter) is phase 3's coverage rule; until it lands, release 1 alone leans
cautious in an even 3v6.
