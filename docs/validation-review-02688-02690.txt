Findings
1. Phone sound: explicitly tick the owned emitter on every unpaused active
   pulse, including silent intervals. The source gate detects owned emitters
   without a tick; the mock emitter verifies that pulses actually call it.
2. Phone answer: offer and accept the action for the entire active call, rather
   than only the two-second ring. Revalidate the exact captured call at both
   click and completion; a later call to the same handset cannot satisfy it.
3. Cracking: choose early versus completed unlock by elapsed cracking progress,
   rather than the number of hidden letters. Real unlock tests cover one, two,
   three and four hidden letters and a completed crack.
4. Needs scope: clear the leader-centred scope independently for emergency
   thirst and hunger. Stopped expedition members skip the remote-player pause
   and distance tests, and use carried supplies for routine needs even when
   membership comes from real commands rather than options. Moving trips defer
   routine needs as before. Test remote Follow orders with carried water and
   food, plus emergencies using an actor-local sink and marked food.
5. Shared pause: a seated follower defers its own routine needs without resetting
   the leader's clock for companions on foot. Leader movement/boarding still
   resets it. Test a standing follower beside a seated follower for 40 seconds.
6. Phone prerequisites: after bounded discovery finds no phone, reselect with
   phone unavailable using the same opening slot, not an automatic word lock.
   Preserve the computer, owner, drawer claim and opening counter. Prepare/open
   the selected method through the shared existing paths; record the actual
   method as seen. Keep the 128-square slice and 4096-square hard limit.
7. Radio schedule: when registering the first request after an idle period,
   anchor lastNight to the current night. Do not reset an existing queue or
   broadcast. Test quiet nights, registration at 14:00, 02:59 and the next 03:00.
8. Missing drawer: clear the computer's drawer reference as well as its ledger
   claim when loaded furniture is definitely absent. Commit the abandoned reward
   so it cannot be recreated. Unloaded locations retain their exact claims.
9. Crowbar: pass duration 300 before native queueing starts, rather than assigning
   it to an already queued action. The regression captures the install argument.
10. USER2 digits: route typed footer numbers through the displayed options, as
    for word and remember locks. Test opening Failed logins with its shown digit.
11. Prompt: share lock-specific placeholder text between boot and phone fallback.
    Test a replacement method with a different prompt from the abandoned phone.
12. Boarding withdrawal: suppress distant combat only with a verified stopped
    car on the same floor, within 12 meters, and a current seat assignment.
    Otherwise retain ordinary combat choices. Test full and entry-busy cars,
    moving/distant cars, another floor, missing/erroring APIs and hostile humans.
    Existing contact, emergency, doctrine and physical boarding tests still pass.
13. Errand boundary: preserve the formation-distance admission rule, but let an
    admitted errand finish within the six-meter source radius plus a two-meter
    interaction margin. Test follow distances 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8, boundary movement,
    actual needs action dispatch and cancellation when the leader moves again.
14. Answer feedback: report success only after a code was really heard. An ended
    or replaced call returns failure with readable redial advice instead.
