Survivor radio watch — 8 November 2026 — living_fellows/0.27.4

Implementation
SCRadioWork owns the radio assignment, measured work time, contact receipt and
checked invitation. It uses the existing base-job save schema, persistent PC
movableData, shared computer interaction/ownership and native actor creation.
No bridge change or additional dependency is required.

Default: four game hours of actual keyboard work; 35% contact chance. One outcome
is retained per completed job, with Invite, Pass and Cancel controls. A recruit
gets a fixed identity before dispatch, joins the roster and approaches safe
outdoor camp ground/perimeter without forcing locked camp doors. Missing power
or hardware retains progress and uses the existing retry controls.

The named operator remains visible on the LARCH/WAVE Survivor Watch page.
Its animated signal display uses the current CRT palette and is a fictional SDR
display, not measured audio or discovery of hidden vanilla broadcast scripts.
Ordinary radio reception and expedition channels remain under their existing
services. Working chatter and discovery lines use the normal dialogue service.

Cost and ownership
No world/computer discovery service was added. The existing radio service checks
only explicit pending invitations and radio receipts. The worker's controller
advances only while its actor owns and performs the keyboard action. The visible
screen uses a 500 ms retained snapshot and 40 drawn bars. Invitation routing tests
at most four candidates, with bounded local welcome-point reads. Closed, unstaffed
computers do no recruitment searching or elapsed-time catch-up.

Automated checks
The existing Behaviour and Interface checks cover named assignment, PC contention,
the actual Decision ownership hold, routine hunger preemption, measured work,
animation renewal, saved progress, power loss/retry, exact job ownership, one roll,
one invitation and conservative interrupted-arrival handling. The UI test checks
that all signal bars remain visible at 1280x720, actions fit, no native polling is
added per rendered frame, and contacts wait for an explicit invitation.

Native acceptance
Disposable LF-only save clones use Project Zomboid 42.21.0 and the isolated source
bridge, one game process at a time, 3 GB heap, four processors and 30 FPS. The
original save and installed launcher are untouched. A real walkie-talkie and UPS
are installed through the normal hardware APIs. Ordinary native typing, the AI
controller, the real CRT renderer and native actor creation/navigation are used.
The fixture seeds a partial shift and forces the final contact roll; this is not
a four-hour natural-balance measurement. Nearby clone-only zombies are cleared
for the final quiet positive-path scenario.

Earlier runs captured blockers before correction: fixture setup before position
settled; a real controller hold preventing progress during typing; insufficient
720p plot space; a native overall-health setter being recalculated from healthy
body parts; a recruit approaching a locked indoor door; and a legitimate camp
alarm pausing work beyond the probe's original timeout. Screenshots/logs are kept.
The production fixes poll the radio controller, use vanilla's weighted general
health reduction plus calculateOverallHealth for stable native weakness,
use an outdoor welcoming point and preserve safety preemption.
