Companion Talk - live verification and approach fix
Living Fellows 0.27.10, Project Zomboid 42.22.2, 13 November 2026.

Found and fixed
The first live attempt exposed an actual conversation approach stall. Dana
walked toward the player, navigation reported arrived, but she remained
outside the 2.8-tile speaking range until the conversation timed out.
Standing candidates were measured at their tile corners, while navigation
targets tile centres and used its ordinary 0.6-tile stopping radius.

SCPositioning now measures candidate centres and reserves room for a
0.15-tile arrival radius within the existing speaking range. Approaches use
the exact destination tile and continuous movement. Sight, collision,
floor, threat and timeout checks remain active. No bridge change is needed.
The existing gameplay regression covers reaching a conversation destination
from six directions and actually invoking the reply callback at its stopping
point, rather than accepting a navigation result alone.
