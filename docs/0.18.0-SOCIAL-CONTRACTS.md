# 0.18.0 Social Contracts

## Player flow

A discovered, non-hostile household appoints one representative. Within speaking
range, the world context menu and translucent Factions panel allow the player to
ask about status, needs, residents, trade, dangers, rumors, and permission to
enter. The representative owns overhead speech; the local player is never used
as the dialogue actor.

Each household may expose one offer and hold at most one active contract. The
initial contract types are:

- supply delivery;
- medical delivery;
- clearing a bounded local threat.

Promises have deadlines and survive save/load. Completion, withdrawal, and
expiry enter bounded history and memory. Success can unlock barter, temporary
guest access, safe-rest permission, more favorable trade, imperfect map
intelligence, and future recruitment consideration. It never recruits a
resident directly from the first agreement.

## Social variation

Household personality, standing, scarcity, crisis state, internal relationships,
and remembered favor affect dialogue and outcomes. Trade reserves are explicit;
under-value offers return a numeric counteroffer. Rumor coordinates improve with
trust and relevant background knowledge but retain at least four tiles of
uncertainty.

One bounded complication may shape an agreement:

- hidden severity;
- diverted delivery;
- a rival resident contesting access;
- a promised reward the household cannot pay;
- private dissent from another resident.

House access is a persistent policy with threshold, lower-weapon, guest, denied,
and contested states. Aiming blocks entry. Theft, damage, barricade tampering,
murder, or hostility revokes access. Contested invitations can be respected or
appealed without bypassing standing and completed-contract gates.

## Scope and safety

0.18.0 does not add faction wars, strategic expansion, radio networks, caravans,
or first-contract recruitment. Social state is household-bounded and capped:
32 completed contracts, 64 memories, and 24 promises. Contract updates run every
2.5 seconds rather than every frame. Local-threat scans are limited to loaded
squares and a maximum 625-square budget. Inventory deliveries and barter use the
existing rollback-safe transaction layer.

The private live sandbox passed representative speech, all three contract types,
all five complications, broken promises, guest-access revocation, improved rumor
precision, persistence, and native hostile targeting on Project Zomboid 42.20.4.
