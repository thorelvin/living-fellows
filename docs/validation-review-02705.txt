Second review: current fix pass
------------------------------
Also reviewed C:\ZOMBOID\codex-handoff-0.27.5-fix-pass.md in full.
The results in this section supersede the first-pass parser snapshot below.
The camp, sorting, recruitment and ownership fixes were retained.

Care detection now uses twelve bounded warning concepts: absence, ending plans,
loss of purpose, prepared means, goodbye acts, disappearance, contemplated
jumping, being better off dead, wanting everything over, no way out, rumination,
and personal wishes to stop existing. Each has two independently worded examples
in the existing conversation test. This is rule-based offline text processing,
only during input; it adds no polling, network call or hosted language model.

Ambiguous own-death references ask a private care_check question before ordinary
intent scoring. A yes enters full care and adds the support notice once in that
session. No and uncertain answers clear the soft question; unrelated input gets
its ordinary handler. Private text and later correction requests stay out of the
missed-message log. Persona and locked-EMMA tests cover the same flow without
unlocking, scoring password arguments or awarding story milestones.

Ordinary game idioms are masked locally, so another genuine disclosure in the
same input can still win. Other subjects, past escape reports, game characters,
routine rope uses and survival-age questions do not become personal death wishes.
The broad deserve/here phrases were scoped to the speaker as requested.

Self put-down predicates now respect negation, approved modifiers, sentence
endings and the am-I question form. Short words match exactly. All worth words
are protected from slang rewriting. Ordinary first-person activity cannot become
an untargeted insult through the fallback's fuzzy scoring.

Borrowed-power phone screens explain the restriction and omit Place the call.
Terminal-open exceptions go to diagnostics; players receive a fixed message.
The exact verified charge rollback is retained. Short knock-knock punchlines
finish the exchange even when they mention jokes or expand into extra tokens;
questions, data readings and checked actions keep their own handlers.
