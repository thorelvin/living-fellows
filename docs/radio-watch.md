# Survivor radio watch

Mount a working two-way walkie-talkie on an unlocked, powered computer inside
camp. Open **Local services → LARCH/WAVE radio desk → Survivor radio watch** and
choose **Assign radio operator**. The list offers companions already on base
duty. A companion finishing another job must finish it first, or you can choose
someone else.

The operator uses a free chair facing the keyboard when one is available,
or works standing if the chair cannot be reached. The radio page shows their
name, saved search progress, a sweeping spectrum, scrolling waterfall and a
rolling operator log. Paused work stops the scan. You can monitor the screen
while they work; its display animation runs only while visible.
This fictional EMMA-assisted search runs entirely
inside the game; ordinary handset channels and expedition reception are retained.

A shift takes four game hours of actual work and has a 35% chance to find a
survivor. Food, drink, rest, medical care and danger take precedence. Power loss
or missing hardware pauses the assignment and preserves its progress, with
automatic retry and the existing manual Retry control in Base. Saving or leaving
the area grants no unattended progress. **Cancel shift** ends that assignment;
assigning another starts a fresh search.

A successful search leaves a named contact on the computer. Choose **Invite
survivor to camp** when ready. The survivor joins your roster and travels toward
camp using ordinary companion navigation, meeting you on safe outdoor camp
ground or its entrance perimeter rather than forcing locked doors. They arrive weak and tired, without
immunity. If the roster is full or a safe loaded arrival route cannot be found,
the contact remains available. An interrupted arrival is checked against its exact
identity and is never blindly duplicated after reload.

Operators have short start, working, no-contact and discovery lines, including
“I found one. He's barely alive.” and its female counterpart. Work chatter uses
the existing dialogue timing and sleep protections.
