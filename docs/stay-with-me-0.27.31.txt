Implementation
--------------

SWMSeatPuppet, the current Bridge.body reference, and the original client,
server and seated-visual identity APIs. LF keeps no ownership-result cache.
Recognised people never become ordinary zombie threats and remain neutral
even under conflicting hostile Bandits/A-Life metadata. The native dispatch
handler rejects melee, firearm, shove and stomp requests before a provider.

SCNativeCompanion also filters its own final native HitInfo list. This covers
incidental bystanders and repeated pellet contacts, independently of LF's
general friendly-fire setting and the age of a perception snapshot. Excluded
entries return to CombatManager's pool. Unmarked API-owned mirrors use the
current Lua adapter through a protected Kahlua call. A failing live callback
cannot authorise damage. No foreign AI functions or zombie navigation flags,
states, paths or waypoints are replaced. Other attackers keep their own lists.
