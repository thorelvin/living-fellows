EMMA body and county calendar - Phase D, 0.27.5
2026-11-09

Implemented
Recovering a clinic, pharmacy or research computer enables readings of the
actual operator's wounds, dressings, needs and infection state. Authority and
media computers recover county schedules. Twelve ordinary fragments recover
either missing index. The operator file and seasonal dawn/dusk are ungated.
Camp records retain their existing access rules.

Wound infection, false-infection symptoms and Knox infection are separate.
The first ordinary login after infection gives one sober warning per episode.
An explicit question can request one approximate mortality estimate. Missing
timing, unknown mortality and nonlethal mortality never invent a deadline.
Care suppresses automatic notices, jokes and countdowns, while permitting
plain, explicit health readings. Optional per-operator warning receipts use
the existing shared-mind bucket without replacing any other data.

Forecasts cover today through six days ahead, with bounded storm/snow/fog/frost
searches. Utility records distinguish future, imminent, past and Never settings,
including an LF-restored grid. A helicopter flight window is explicitly not
proof of an aircraft overhead. Short follow-ups refresh the relevant reading.
Facts live only within their response; conversation stores a bounded topic,
not an actor, old measurement or permanent medical record.

No background service, event hook, global wrapper or bridge change was added.
Reads run at foreground login or on request. Body parts are capped at 32,
moodles at 24, skills at 128, traits at 64 and forecasts at seven days.
