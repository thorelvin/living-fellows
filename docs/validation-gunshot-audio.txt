Companion gunshot audio
Branch: living_fellows/0.26.88
Game: Project Zomboid 42.21.0

Cause
The existing companion sound shim handled PlaySwingSound animation events.
The installed ranged handgun/rifle animation nodes send AttackCollisionCheck
with Shot, but no PlaySwingSound event. Vanilla starts firearm audio in
ISReloadWeaponAction.attackHook, through playRangedWeaponShootSound. Companion
attacks start through the native DoAttack path, bypassing that Lua input hook.
Consequently an actual round and bullet impact could occur without its report.

Change
- Observe native ammunition before and after a completed collision callback.
  Read the magazine, chamber and retained spent-cartridge counts, including the
  last chambered round, pump actions and revolvers.
- For a confirmed firearm shot, play the weapon's configured SwingSound through
  the companion's native playRangedWeaponShootSound method and positional emitter.
- Preserve the PlayedSwingSound latch so duplicate animation layers or a modded
  firing animation cannot add a second report to that shot.
- Reject missing/stale evidence, a different weapon, an already jammed gun,
  unchanged ammunition and dry fire. A feeding jam after firing must not mute
  the round that already fired. Unlimited-ammo shots retain their audio.
- Audio errors stay cosmetic. Do not replay attacks, modify ammunition, apply
  damage, change player ownership or add duplicate zombie-attraction events.
- Rebuild and package the native bridge with the source change.
