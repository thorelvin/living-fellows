Living Fellows: bounded camp supply discovery
2026-11-03; living_fellows/0.26.92 working changes.

Report and finding
The tester confirms version 0.26.92. No current tester console, save or frame
capture is available. The local console is dated October 31 and the local loose
installation is 0.26.86; neither establishes the tester's live cause.

Released .92 has a reproducible excessive scan: findMarkedSupply examines up to
256 items PER marked container, with no total item/container/time allowance.
It visits unrelated storage categories even when food is immediately available.
The scheduler checks its deadline between callbacks and cannot interrupt this
loop. The per-container limit dates to .80; .88 extended food discovery across
camp floors, amplifying this cost in large multi-storey camps.

The inspected .92 computer paths do not resume an idle world-discovery service:
persona discovery is first-boot work; building controls require an open terminal
and closing pauses the building scan. The fix adds no background service.

Production fix
- Retain each query's storage/item cursor across work slices. A partial search
  stays pending and cannot authorize emergency food or declare supplies missing.
- Limit each request to 64 inspected items and two container resolutions.
  All companions share 128 items and four containers per scheduler frame.
  Refund unused quota; honor the scheduler's remaining frame time.
- Yield cooperatively at 0.75 ms between item/container operations. A single
  native lookup cannot be preempted; this is not a hard frame-time guarantee.
- Check food/water categories first and nearer stores within each category.
  Other permitted categories remain searchable for misfiled supplies.
- Share up to eight positive candidates per camp/query for two seconds. Recheck
  current storage membership, withdrawal permissions, exact item/container,
  actor predicate, personal protection, reserves and claims before selection.
  No candidate actor references or persistent save data are introduced.
- Handle moved items, changed containers and a shrinking source without losing
  a shifted candidate. Unknown/unloaded stores remain pending. A busy known
  matching source does not cause repeated prefix scans.
- Interleave pending water and food discovery for hungry/thirsty companions;
  an actual supply errand still keeps its existing ownership.
- At camp, a missing marked supply does not trigger a second broad square scan.
  Ordinary outside-camp nearby supply behavior is retained.
- Skip redundant reserve counts for zero-reserve stock; use exact counts for
  protected quantities rather than the old first-80-item availability sample.
