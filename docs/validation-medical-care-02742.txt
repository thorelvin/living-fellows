Implemented
  Display rows retain healed dressings, stitches, splints and lodged objects;
  active injury counters exclude healed dressings and spent splints. Hidden
  native timers supplement masked injury flags through both capture paths.
  Stitch readiness is supplied only to viewers with First Aid 7 or higher.
  Internal severity scores remain private to treatment selection.

  Existing self/current-helper treatment claims remove stitches at time >=40
  and splints only at verified fracture time 0. Covered stitches wait for an
  exact fresh dressing. Safety, contact and player-care checks apply at commit.
  Committed removal is never replayed. Pending redressing and material returns
  survive cancellation; external dressing or verified healed skin can resolve
  redressing without spending another dressing. Minor wounds prefer plain
  dressings before sterile or alcohol supplies.

  Personal washing handles up to eight carried dressings with native actions
  and original-bag returns. Camp wash_dressings jobs fetch permitted stock and
  move exact ownership receipts to verified native replacements, then deposit
  them into currently permitted Medical storage. Carried-water washing pins
  the dirty input and bottle, prefers tainted water, protects loans and personal
  items, and preserves 1 L when consuming usable drinking water.

  An old bridge is an environmental restore wait, preserving the complete saved
  companion without consuming restore attempts or quarantining the record.
