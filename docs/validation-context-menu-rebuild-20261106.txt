Implemented

Clicked companions have direct Talk, Follow, Stay and Retreat. Remaining person
actions, nearby companions, Squad, Camp, vehicles, generators, Households and
Strange Folk use separate native submenu branches. Camp Storage is last.
Routine radio services, computer maintenance and ordinary map planning are
grouped; pending radio replies and active map choices remain direct.

Callbacks retain the displayed actor/item/object identity and revalidate it.
Basic orders and Watch preserve the panel selection. Send Home preserves the
reviewed group membership and requires renewed review when membership changes.
Native Medical Check is retained without a duplicate LF health entry.

Popup facts avoid full panel descriptions, repeated roster enumeration and cold
command-state initialization. Probes do not discover cleaning supplies or routes.
No background service was added. Native submenu slots are reused without moving
foreign entries. LF background styling is scoped to rendering, so pooled foreign
menus retain their own appearance. World targets use the original requestX/Y,
even when native placement shifts a menu away from the edge of the screen.

complete gameplay pass. Physical mouse/controller use, companion movement,
pending mission decisions, every household and every Strange Folk story were
not exercised live. Relevant ownership/structure paths have automated coverage.

Computer maintenance appeared in the first successful native capture. In the
final run the relocated observer was too far from that computer, so its missing
maintenance branch is recorded rather than counted as a live pass. Automated
computer-context checks pass. The map opens through native ShowWorldMap for this
component capture; it does not prove the timed map-reading action.

then a missing local in optional timing instrumentation. Both stopped safely;

native pass missed the map because it did not await map reading, and exposed the
mutable-click-coordinate bug subsequently fixed and regression tested.
