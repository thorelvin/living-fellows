======================================================
Base: 47024196 (0.27.24). Project Zomboid 42.22.2, single-player, Living Fellows only.
Bridge protocol 42.22-isocompanion-31 and save schemas unchanged; Lua only.

Problem (0.27.24 follow-up)
  Two guards holding a house door against twelve zombies took 9 s and 15.5 s
  to fall back in two 0.27.24 runs, once with 2 bites. 0.27.23 left in 2.5 s
  only because it fled from any door with three zombies at it.
  Instrumented run SC-Harness-20261116-133606-86682525: each guard saw 11
  zombies beyond the door while the squad forecast counted 5 to 6. The
  forecast counts zombies within 4.5 m plus arrivals with a time to reach
  anyone; a queue standing a few metres outside has neither. The squad mode
  flickered between engage and disengage, and every flicker restarted the
  2.5 s count toward giving up the post.

Changes (SCGuardPost.lua, SCCombat.lua, SCConfig.lua)
  1. Post.doorQueue: zombies a door guard sees in or beyond the opening within
     10 m. Post.observe treats a queue larger than the squad's capacity plus
     the disengage margin as a pull-out signal, and capacity plus 4 as an
     immediate one (the same lines the squad forecast uses).
  2. Squad pull-out signals keep counting through a calm shorter than
     guardChokeCalmMs (1000 ms). A guard's own retreats restart their count as
     soon as they stop, as before. (A first version let personal retreats
     accumulate too; live it gave up an ordinary 2v3 door, so it was split.)
  3. A door guard's retreat never picks a square beyond its door (escape
     squares, the shared retreat square and route fallbacks). Live run
     SC-Harness-20261116-134516-84bf3969 lost a guard that retreated out
     through the door into the queue.
  Tried and reverted: a straight or diagonal step back for a front guard with
  a zombie in the opening. It let zombies into the post square or past the
  guard; the door hold keeps 0.27.24's hold-still behaviour.

Correction to the 0.27.24 write-up
  docs/horde-last-part-0.27.24.txt reported the ordinary doorway case (2
  guards v 3) clean in three runs. It is not reliably clean. An A/B on
  unmodified 0.27.24 game code with today's probe failed it in all three runs
  (2 kills, one guard dead with 2 bites, 1 kill). Across all runs today it
  passed 4 of 7 on 0.27.24 code and 3 of 12 on 0.27.25 builds (including
  builds with since-removed changes). Failure modes seen: the front guard is
  pushed out of the door line and a zombie then stands on its spot, so the
  step back into line is refused; zombies slip past into the room; a guard
  holding still with a zombie in the opening is bitten. This remains open.
  It was already worse on 0.27.23, which abandoned the door at once.
