Collect fuel

Choose Expeditions > Collect fuel, or select a gas station on your explored map.
Fuel collection requires a car in marked parking and a camp. Automatic selection
uses known stations within the normal 5,000-tile driving limit.

Choose Full load, 1/2/4/8/12/24 cans, or Top up car only. Before departure the crew
packs permitted empty and partly filled gasoline cans from camp storage or the
car. Full cans and protected supplies stay home. Filled-can weight, the worker's
carrying space and trunk capacity limit the load; Full load is capped at 256 cans.
Missing cans pause preparation rather than changing a collection trip to car-only.
Top up car only still brings one utility can for shuttling fuel if the car cannot
stop beside the pump; it does not request additional filled cargo.

The car is filled first. When it cannot stop beside the pump, a crew member
shuttles gasoline in a can. Cans are then filled and loaded one at a time. Grid
power is preferred. With the grid off, the crew can use a suitable outdoor
generator already at the station, connect it and add starter fuel if necessary.
They may prepare up to two litres of starter fuel at home from permitted stock,
returning the exact donor can before departure. They do not bring a generator.

A generator the crew starts is stopped before leaving; a connection they add is
removed. An already running or connected generator retains its original state.
If immediate danger prevents cleanup, the report warns that inspection is needed.
Automatic trips may try up to three stations. A map-selected trip stays at your
chosen station and can try its other pumps.

The expedition reports pumped litres, fuel added to the car, generator use,
carried fuel and stored fuel separately. Driving home consumes real tank fuel.
Returning alone does not count cans as delivered. The stopped car is unloaded
into permitted Tools, then General storage, regardless of the restock setting.
Full or inaccessible storage pauses delivery with the actual cargo preserved;
automatic retry runs after three minutes, and Retry remains available.
An unresolved can receipt does not prevent delivery of other verified cans.

Uses native Lua timed actions, actual pump stock, gasoline and item identities.
No added background service or native bridge protocol change. Fuel trips use
expedition save schema 10 and delivery integration version 3; older trips retain
their existing formats. Ambiguous missing cargo is never recreated.
Crew inventories retain exact native item IDs until fuel delivery is settled,
including saves during refuelling, carrying, loading and paused home delivery.
