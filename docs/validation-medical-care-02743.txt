Implemented scope
  One ordered medical pipeline for self-care and helping another patient:
  undressed bleeding, lodged bullet, lodged glass, unsplinted fracture, burn
  cleaning, local wound disinfection, open wounds, active dirty dressings,
  ready stitches and spent splints.

  Bullet/glass removal requires usable permitted tweezers or a needle holder.
  No bare-handed glass removal. A fresh exact dressing is reserved before
  uncovering a part. Burn cleaning uses two separate clean dressings: one
  consumed by cleaning, another applied afterwards. Native effects are verified
  before exact supply debits. Committed work retains its unfinished obligations
  rather than repeating surgery. Ambiguous native inventory outcomes quarantine
  their evidence instead of generating compensating items.

  Disinfection treats local wound infection only. It prefers wipes, disinfectant,
  rubbing alcohol and then spare strong spirits. Native strength and amount
  thresholds apply, with 0.15 L fluid doses. Protected, loaned, manually equipped,
  held and drinking reserves are excluded. Repetition requires native alcohol
  exhausted and at least two actual game hours since the verified dose.

  Camp visits retain patientId independently of assigned medic. One open job
  per patient survives waiting and recovery. Scoped holds require an actual
  medical owner and current safety; player care remains separate. Busy/no medic
  yields the same job to self-care after the bounded wait. Leaving duty or camp
  prevents new elective work. Streaming absence does not erase recovery proof.
