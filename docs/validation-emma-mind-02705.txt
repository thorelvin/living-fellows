Scope
World ModData LF_EmmaMindV1 holds bounded operator records, explicit notes,
one-time computer receipts and domain indexes. Local short conversations and
care dialogue are excluded. Ordinary EMMA contributes once; locked PCs wait
for an unlocked boot. Persona machines remain excluded after factory reset.
Legacy notes migrate once without rewriting their source. Overflow stays on
the original computer until room is available. Factory reset affects that
computer, preserving the shared notebook, fragments and operator recognition.

Generic "forget everything" lists notes and asks which to erase. Specific
or all-note deletion uses fresh, one-use Y/N tickets with revision and content
checks. Progress reads "Computer recovery: 1 fragment recovered" and "Next
memory tier at 2 fragments", with the correct singular/plural.

Jump-starting requires the primary operator, Electrical 2, a screwdriver and
an exact, owned, intact car battery with at least 5% charge. The native timed
installation action revalidates on completion and subtracts .05 once. A
foreground lease lasts 600000 real milliseconds or until logoff; no refund
for early logoff, no persisted lease and no background drain. Normal supply
is required for radio endpoints/work, discovery, building authorization,
security routines and background password-recovery credit. No new event hook,
idle scan, global action wrapper or native bridge code was added.
