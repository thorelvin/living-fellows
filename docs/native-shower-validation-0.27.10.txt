Native clothing removal and showers - 0.27.10
Project Zomboid 42.22.2, 13 November 2026. Lua-only follow-up.

Implementation
Automatic mask removal uses stock ISUnequipAction effects and animations.
Existing manual-wear/protection checks remain in force. Its constructor's
non-hotbar fields are initialized on the stock class without accessing the
main player's hotbar. Instance callbacks omit player UI/attachment operations.

Showers natively undress and dress one item at a time. Receipts retain the
original item identities, worn slots, held equipment and activated wearables.
The wear effect's slot delegate is synchronous and instance-local. Normal
completion is reported only after dressing and native ownership release.
Danger, orders and cancellation restore available gear through stock effects
immediately, once native cancellation releases the actor. Pending cleanup
retains its receipt. New player equipment choices and missing/broken items
are respected; no missing item is recreated.

Snapshots no longer cancel a shower to restore its outfit. They reference
temporarily removed equipment already present in that actor's captured item
tree, in its original slots. Actual quantities and consumable/fluid states
remain captured normally. No save schema or bridge change is required.

Automated checks
Stock wear/unequip Lua: exact inputs, one commit, actor isolation, cancellation,
original-slot restoration, activated wearable recovery, changed equipment,
missing items and delayed native cleanup. The existing native-care suite also
continues to cover washing, drying, repair and real crafting outputs.
